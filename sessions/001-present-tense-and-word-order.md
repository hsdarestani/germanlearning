## Introduced by tutor
1. Present tense regular verb `lernen`: ich lerne; du lernst; er/sie/es lernt; wir lernen; ihr lernt; sie/Sie lernen.
2. In ordinary independent declarative sentences the conjugated verb is in the second position: `Ich lerne Deutsch.`
3. For basic yes/no questions the verb starts: `Lernst du Deutsch?`
4. Short beginner phrases and spelling/capitalization conventions.
5. Corrected placement examples: `er hat` (not `er habe`); `wir lernen` (not `wir lerne`).
